## Tech Debt

### Phase 13: Blocking & Unmatch
- **W1 (MOD-02, MOD-03).** On WebSocket connect, `ChatService.deliverUnreadMessages` pushes previews of messages that were undelivered when the block or unmatch happened. It runs no block or ended-match check, so a hidden, locked conversation can still raise notifications. *Fix:* skip conversations whose match has `endedAt != null` or whose pair is `isBlockedEitherWay`, and mark those messages delivered or leave them. This is the item most worth closing before launch, since it sits on the milestone's core safety promise.

### Phase 15: Age Verification
- **W2 (AGE-03).** Accounts without a DOB before V22 can't set one and stay out of discovery. This isn't a regression, but there's no path to recovery.

### Phase 16: Invite-Only Access & Referral
- **W3 (WAIT-04, low).** An invite from a waitlist conversion isn't bound to the waitlist email, by design (bearer code).

### Phase 17: Waitlist / Landing-Page API
- **Before launch:** confirm the production reverse-proxy shape, and set `RATE_LIMIT_TRUSTED_PROXIES` and `WAITLIST_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`. This is the UAT test deferred to deploy.
- Invite email not yet checked in a real client.
- Open review warnings WR-01 to WR-08. The ones with the most user-facing impact are WR-03 (misleading dead-link copy), WR-04 (waitlist-spot hijack through normalization and re-join), WR-06 (invite delivery failures swallowed) and WR-08 (admin PII endpoint has no brute-force throttling).
- Open review info items IN-01 to IN-07. IN-01 is the coincidental reliance on the default StrictHttpFirewall.
- Disposition bookkeeping: re-add the two dropped warnings (unbounded bucket maps; about 72 mails/day per victim) under new IDs.
- Not yet checked: a stale or invalid Bearer header on admin endpoints may be rejected by the JWT filter before AdminTokenGuard runs.

**Total:** 3 integration warnings, plus 1 deploy check, 8 open review warnings, 7 info items, 2 lost dispositions and 2 follow-ups, across 4 phases.
