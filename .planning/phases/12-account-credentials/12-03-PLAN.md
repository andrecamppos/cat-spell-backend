<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| authenticated client → service | userId comes from the verified JWT principal; currentPassword/newEmail are untrusted input |
| public token → confirm path | rawToken is the sole proof of ownership at confirm time |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-03-01 | Elevation of Privilege | changePassword / requestChange | high | mitigate | Re-verify current password (passwordEncoder.matches) before any credential mutation; wrong password -> 403, no state change. |
| T-12-03-02 | Tampering | confirmEmailChange token claim | high | mitigate | Atomic markUsed (used_at IS NULL) claim; 0 rows -> InvalidTokenException, so the email swaps at most once even under concurrent confirm. |
| T-12-03-03 | Spoofing / account takeover | email swap on confirm | high | mitigate | Identity change revokes ALL refresh sessions and mints no tokens (D-08) forcing fresh login. |
| T-12-03-04 | Denial of Service | inbox bombing a victim address | medium | mitigate | Per-target-new_email Bucket4j guard throttles confirm-email sends to a given address. |
| T-12-03-05 | Information Disclosure | 409 on taken email | low | accept | D-06 explicitly accepts the 409 on an authenticated, rate-limited caller; broader account enumeration is canon — covered by /gsd-secure-phase, not minted here. |
</threat_model>
