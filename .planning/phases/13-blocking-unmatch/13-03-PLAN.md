<objective>
Fan the bidirectional block predicate + ended-match lock out to the remaining read/send surfaces so the
block is enforced everywhere (Pitfall 1): profile-detail 404 + swipe match-lookup 404 in DiscoveryService
(D-01, D-03), chat send guard on both entry paths + message-open guard in ChatService (D-02), and hide ended
(blocked/unmatched) conversations from both users' lists while retaining history server-side (D-04, D-05). The
discovery-feed surface was already enforced at the query layer in Plan 13-01; this plan completes the other
three surfaces (including guarding the swipe path so a blocked pair can neither form nor reactivate a match)
and proves all four with an enforcement integration test.

Purpose: Guarantee that neither party of a blocked pair can see or contact the other on ANY surface, that a
blocked-or-unmatched conversation is locked + hidden with history retained, and that unmatch (unlike block)
lets the pair reappear in the feed.
Output: DiscoveryService profile guards, ChatService send/open guards + list hiding, ConversationRepository
active-only list query, BlockEnforcementIntegrationTest.
</objective>
