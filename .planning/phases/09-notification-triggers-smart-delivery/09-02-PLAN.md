| Backend → FCM | Payload (incl. lock-screen preview) crosses to Google FCM and the device |

| T-9-05 | Information Disclosure | Message preview on lock screen (D-01) | medium | accept | Accepted product decision (CONTEXT.md D-01, dating-app norm). Revisit if per-type toggles / content-hiding ship (deferred). |
| T-9-06 | Tampering | PushPayload.data deep-link keys | low | mitigate | Keys are server-derived from persisted IDs (conversationId/messageId/senderId/matchId); no client input flows into the payload. |
</threat_model>
