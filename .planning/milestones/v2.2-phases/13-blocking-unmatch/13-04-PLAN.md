<objective>
Expose the moderation primitives over HTTP: a `BlockController` with POST/DELETE `/api/blocks/{targetUserId}`
(block/unblock, D-10/D-12) and GET `/api/blocks` (own minimal block list, D-13), plus a DELETE
`/api/matches/{targetUserId}` unmatch endpoint on the existing `MatchController` (MOD-05). Every action is
scoped to the JWT principal (IDOR-safe) and blocking works by bare userId from any context (D-10). An
endpoint integration test proves 204/400/404 behavior, idempotency, IDOR scoping, and that unmatch does not
ban rediscovery.

Purpose: Give clients the four user-protection operations (block, unblock, view list, unmatch) with RFC 7807
error bodies and no block disclosure to the target.
Output: `moderation/controller/BlockController.kt`, MatchController unmatch endpoint, BlockEndpointIntegrationTest.
</objective>
