  <behavior>
    - change-email to a taken address -> 409, no email sent, no pending row.
    - change-email to an available address -> 202 ack; a confirm email is sent to the NEW address; users.email is unchanged until confirm.
    - confirm with the captured token -> 200; users.email now equals the new address, email_verified_at is stamped, and pre-existing sessions are revoked.
    - confirm with a reused token -> rejected (401/invalid), email not swapped again.
    - confirm with an unknown/expired token -> rejected, no swap.
  </behavior>
  <action>
    Add the change-email cases to `AccountCredentialsIntegrationTest`. Register a second
    user to own a "taken" address; assert `POST /api/auth/change-email` to that address
    returns 409 and that `sentMessages` gained no confirm message for it. For the happy
    path: call change-email to a fresh address, assert 202, extract the confirm token
    from the message whose `to` equals the NEW address (regex `token=([A-Za-z0-9_-]+)`
    over `textBody`), and assert (via a `/me`-authenticated call or DB lookup) that the
    account email has NOT changed yet. Then `POST /api/auth/confirm-email-change` with the
    token, assert 200, and assert the account email is now the new address, that
    logging in with the new email + password succeeds, and that a refresh token minted

    past via the repository) rejection cases, asserting no further email swap.
  </action>
