## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-02-01 | Information Disclosure | EmailChangeEmailRenderer recipient | high | mitigate | Renderer targets the caller-supplied recipient (the pending new_email) only; it never renders to or discloses the account's current email. |
| T-12-02-02 | Spoofing | 403 vs 401 semantics on authenticated request | medium | mitigate | Distinct 403 INVALID_CURRENT_PASSWORD avoids a 401 on an authenticated request that could confuse token-refresh logic (D-02). |
| T-12-02-03 | Information Disclosure | ProblemDetail body | low | accept | Detail message is a generic "Current password is incorrect"; no account data echoed. Account-existence enumeration is canon — covered by /gsd-secure-phase; not minted here. |
</threat_model>
